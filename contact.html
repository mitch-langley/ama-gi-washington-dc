Interviews, Collaborations, & Discussions

I am available for press interviews, collaborative articles, knowledge contribution, and conversations regarding topics of interest. Whether you are developing an in-depth analytical piece, seeking background context on industry trends, looking to co-author a publication, or simply interested in one of my viewpoints, I welcome the opportunity to connect.


While I am very conversational, I kindly request that initial contact be channeled through my e-mail, after which we can coordinate a phone or in-person conversation.

Media and Interviews

For press inquiries, time-sensitive interview requests, or background briefings, please reach out directly with your outlet, topic scope, and deadline. I can accommodate written responses, recorded audio/video, or live discussions depending on scheduling.

Collaborative Writing

If you are interested in co-authoring an article, developing an op-ed, contributing to a joint case study, or collaborating on technical and regulatory research, please share a brief summary of the proposed concept, target audience, and prospective platform.
