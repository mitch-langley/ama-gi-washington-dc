Appendix B: Private Libraries in the Viceroyalty of Peru

The number in [straight brackets] refers to the book's numbered assignment within its library.

  1. Dr. Agustín Valenciano de Quiñones · 1576

    A prominent physician and scholar of Leonese origin, his library held 235 books including 7 Salamancan authors.

    • [79] Domingo de Soto. De natura et gracia.
    • [121] Diego de Covarrubias y Leiva. In librum quartum Decretalium epitome.
    • [122] Diego de Covarrubias y Leiva. Practicarum quaestionum liber.
    • [212] Domingo de Soto. Commentaria in quartum Sententiarum.
    • [213] Diego de Covarrubias y Leiva. De Justitia et jure.
    • [214] Tomás de Mercado. Summa de tratos y contratos.
    • [234] Diego de Covarrubias y Leiva. Omnia opera.

  2. Dr. Gregorio González de Cuenca · 1581

    Oidor of the Real Audiencia of Lima, corregidor of Cuzco, and Governor of the General Captaincy of Santo Domingo from 1577 until his death, his library held 213 books including 14 Salamancan authors.

    • [79] Luis de Molina. De Hispanorum primogeniorum origine ac natura.
    • [113] Martín de Azpilcueta. Commento en romance, a manera de repetición Latina y scholástica de juristas, sobre el capítulo Inter verba.
    • [148] Cristóbal de Villalón. Provechoso tratado de cambios y contrataciones de mercaderes y reprovación de usura.
    • [149] Luis Saravia de la Calle. Instrución de mercaderes muy provechosa.
    • [164] Martín de Azpilcueta. Relectio sive iterata praelectio cap. accepta, de restit. spoliat.
    • [166] Martín de Azpilcueta. Relectio cap. Ita quorundam, de Judaeis, in qua de rebus ad Sarracenos deferri, prohibitis et censuris ob id latis non segniter disputatur.
    • [169] Alonso de Castro. De potestate legis poenalis, libri duo.
    • [175] Francisco de Vitoria. Relectiones theologicae.
    • [177] Diego de Covarrubias y Leiva. Omnia opera.
    • [178] Alfonso de Castro. De justa haereticorum punitione, libri tres.
    • [194] Juan de Medina. De restitutione et contractibus tractatus.
    • [196] Domingo de Soto. De ratione tegendi et detegendi secretum.
    • [197] Domingo de Soto. De Justitia et jure, libri decem.
    • [198] Domingo de Soto. Commentaria in quartum Sententiarum.

  3. Antonio Dávalos · 1582

    A colonial official who served as treasurer of the Royal Treasury at Rímac, his library held 75 books including 4 Salamancan authors.

    • [68] Alonso de Orozco. Historia de la reyna Saba quando disputó con el rey Salomón en Hierusalem.
    • [69] Alonso de Orozco. Cathecismo prouechoso.
    • [74] Alonso de Orozco. Epistolario Cristiano para todos estados.
    • [75] Bartolomé de Albornoz. Arte de los contractos.

  4. Fr. Alonso de Torres Maldonado · 1591

    A priest born in Andalucía who focused on teaching Christian doctrine and is especially remembered for his library, including 50 books and 4 Salamancan authors.

    • [2] Domingo de Soto. Commentaria in quartum Sententiarum.
    • [11] Francisco de Vitoria. Summa sacramentorum ecclesiae.
    • [16] Martín de Azpilcueta. Compendio y summario de confesores y penitentes.
    • [34] Domingo de Soto. Summulae.

  5. Hernando Arias de Ugarte · 1614

    A magistrate, official, and priest born in Bogotá who was bishop of Quito, archbishop of Bogotá, archbishop of La Plata, and archbishop of Lima; his library held 417 books including 8 Salamancan authors.

    • [93] Martín de Azpilcueta. Compendium manualis de quaestionibus.
    • [106] Martín de Azpilcueta. Obras de Navarro en tres tomos.
    • [107] Domingo de Soto. Obras en tres tomos.
    • [108] Juan de Medina. Codex de restitutione et contractibus.
    • [122] Cristóbal de Villalón. Provechoso tratado de cambios y contrataciones de mercaderes.
    • [188] Alfonso de Castro. De potestate legis poenalis.
    • [336] Diego Covarrubias de Leiva. Omnia opera.
    • [337] Luis de Molina. De Hispanorum primogeniis.

  6. Colegio Mérida · Jesuit Library, Nueva Granada · 1749-1780

    Founded in 1628 or 1629, this school - considered the first elementary and middle school in what is now Venezuela - formed part of a Jesuit school circuit throughout northern South America; its library held 883 books including 31 Salamancan authors.

    • [47] Martín de Azpilcueta. Manual de confesores y penitentes.

    • [455] Luis de Molina. De justicia.
