Production as Deterrence: The Western Hemisphere's Role in a Future Pacific Fight

Working Paper

Recently, I visited the COSCO-operated megaport at Chancay, Peru, to examine how China’s expanding port infrastructure intersects with a deeper American vulnerability: the absence of a secure industrial rear for a future Pacific conflict. This working paper argues that the Joint Force will not overcome contested logistics and the tyranny of distance in PACOM without integrating trusted Latin American and Caribbean partners into a hemispheric defense-production network. Consistent with the 2025 National Security Strategy, the 2026 National Defense Strategy, and emerging joint concepts, it proposes a program of hemispheric industrial reconstruction combining DFC financing, expanded use of DPA Title III authorities, private capital, procurement reform, and sustained demand generated through deeper military cooperation and the development of shared capability requirements.
